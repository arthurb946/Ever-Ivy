How we work

Three acts. One story.

I

Before

We spend time understanding your story before we ever pick up a camera. Your venue, your people, your pace — we learn it all so on the day, we're invisible.

II

During

We don't interrupt. We observe. Every laugh, every glance, every quiet moment between the big ones — we're watching and we're capturing without you ever noticing.

III

After

Your gallery arrives within six weeks. Edited with care, sequenced like a film. Not hundreds of similar frames — a curated story, beginning to end.

A Featured Story

Sophie & James

Cotswolds, England — Summer 2024

A garden party in the Cotswolds

Sophie and James wanted something unhurried. A long afternoon in the garden of a farmhouse that's been in her family for three generations. Wildflowers. Linen tablecloths. Children running barefoot.

We arrived at dawn. By the time the first guests arrived, we'd already found every corner of light. When Sophie walked into the garden, we were ready — and she never had to know.
